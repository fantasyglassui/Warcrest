# PvP Kills and the Wanted List

When a guild member kills an enemy player, the whole guild hears about it, and the best killers climb the monthly **Top Head Hunters** board.

## How a kill counts

- **You have to attack.** A kill counts only if you hit the enemy (a damaging spell, melee, shooting, or your pet) in the 10 seconds before they died. Being in the party or just targeting them isn't enough.
- **Killing blows and assists.** Whoever hit last gets the killing blow; everyone else who helped gets an assist.
- **No grey kills.** Kills of grey, much lower-level players are never announced or logged, so farming lowbies doesn't climb the board.

The game doesn't tell addons who landed the killing blow, so the addon works it out from what it can see. Turn on **enemy nameplates** (V) so it can see the fight.

## Top Head Hunters

The **Kills** tab lists every kill as a table (killer, victim, where and when) with assists under the zone. On the right, a card shows who's **leading** and by how much, and the **Top Head Hunters** board ranks guild members by killing blows, with a bar for each place. Hover a name on the board to see their assists.

- Names cut short? Drag the thin borders between the **Killer**, **Victim** and **Where** headings to widen a column, or double-click a border to fit the longest name. Making the window bigger (drag its bottom-right corner) gives the table more room too.
- Pick the period at the top: **This month**, **Last month**, **7 days** or **All time**. Only world PvP counts: kills in battlegrounds and arenas are left out.
- The board **starts fresh on the 1st of each month** (realm time).
- On reset day, the guild hears last month's **top three**, and last month's champion is shown at the bottom of the board.

## Kill streaks and revenge

A **streak** is a guild member's killing blows in a row without dying to an enemy player, with no more than an hour between kills.

- The guild hears when someone reaches **3, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30** kills in a row, and each has a name: Killing Spree (3), Rampage (5), Dominating (10), Unstoppable (15), Godlike (20), Legendary (25) and Beyond Godlike (30).
- When a streak of **5 or more** is ended, the guild hears who ended it.
- The Kills tab shows the **best streak** of the period you picked, under Top Head Hunters.

**Revenge:** when a guild member kills an enemy player who killed a guildmate in the last 30 minutes, the guild hears about it.

Turn these off for yourself in **Settings** (Streaks and revenge). Officers can turn them off for the whole guild.

## Player cards

Click a killer on the Kills tab, or a name on Top Head Hunters, to open their **player card**: kills this month and all time, assists, deaths, current and best streak, their **nemesis** (the enemy player who has killed them most) and who they've killed most, plus their DKP, attendance and loot. You can open cards from the Roster, DKP and Raids tabs too, or type `/warcrest card Name`.

## The Wanted list

Enemy players who keep killing guild members in world PvP (3 times in 30 days by default) go on the **Wanted** tab.

- When anyone in the guild sees a wanted player, everyone gets an alert, and the spot is marked on the world map with a purple diamond for 30 minutes.
- **Kill a wanted player and the bounty is claimed:** they come off the list until they gank enough again.
- Right-click someone to hide them from your own list.

## Bounties

Bounties are **off** until a guild leader or officer turns them on (Settings > Alerts > Bounties, then Save for whole guild). Once they're on:

- Officers put a reward on an enemy player's head from the Wanted tab's **Bounties** view (up to 1000 DKP, or no reward if the guild doesn't use DKP), with an optional note.
- The whole guild hears about it. The first guild member to land the killing blow on that player in the next 30 days claims it and is paid automatically. Kills count the same way as on the Kills tab.
- Officers can withdraw a bounty that hasn't been claimed. Claiming a bounty earns the **Collector** achievement.

## Deaths on the map

When a guild member dies in world PvP, an X (or a skull) marks the spot on the world map, including inside OpenMap. Hover a mark for details; click it to set a TomTom waypoint if you use TomTom.
