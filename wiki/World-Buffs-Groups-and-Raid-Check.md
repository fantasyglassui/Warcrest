# World Buffs, Groups and the Raid Check

Five helpers for the things a guild does every week.

## World buffs and world bosses

**Events > World buffs** shows the last time each world buff was dropped today, and the world bosses guildmates have seen.

- **World buffs:** Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer (the Onyxia or Nefarian head), Warchief's Blessing (Rend's head) and Spirit of Zandalar (the Heart of Hakkar). When a guildmate with Warcrest gets one just as it drops, the guild hears where and when, and the view shows how long it lasts. Warcrest goes by the buff itself, not the NPC's yell, so it works in every language.
- **World bosses:** Azuregos, Lord Kazzak, Ysondre, Lethon, Emeriss and Taerar. When a guildmate sees one (target, focus, mouseover or nameplate), the guild hears where, and whether it was up or dead.
- **Alert me** (top right of the World buffs card) turns the banners off just for you. The alert has its own sound in **Settings > Sounds**.
- Drops and sightings are kept for a day. Guildmates who log in later catch up from those who were online.

## Groups

**Guild > Groups** is a board for finding a group inside the guild.

1. Under **Your group**, write what it's for (like *Scholomance*), pick how many you need, tick the roles you want, and click **Post my group**.
2. Guildmates see it under **Open groups**. They pick their role (top right) and click **Ask to join**.
3. You see who asked under **Asking to join**, and click **Invite**.

Nothing goes in chat, and nothing is sent without a click. A post ends after an hour, when you click **Close my group**, or when you log off.

## Profession cooldowns

**Guild > Crafters > Cooldowns** lists who has a transmute, Mooncloth, Cured Rugged Hide or a Salt Shaker, and when each is ready (ready ones first). Each member's addon reads their own cooldowns from the game and shares them when they change, so they show once that member has logged in with Warcrest.

## Raid check

Click **Raid check** at the top of **Raids** before the first pull. It lists each raid (or party) member's flask, elixirs, food and world buffs, the least prepared first. The buffs are read straight from the game, so members without Warcrest show too. The game hides buffs during combat, so check between pulls. **Check again** reads them again.

## Inactive players, with their alts

**Officers > Officer log > Inactive** lists players who haven't logged in for the time you pick. Alts linked to a main (see [DKP for Members](DKP-for-Members)) count with it: a player only shows as away when none of their characters has been online, and their last raid counts every character. Alts show as +1, +2 next to the main's name, and by name in the tooltip.
