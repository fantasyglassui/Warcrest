# Achievements and Titles

The **Achievements** tab shows what you've earned and what you're working towards. Achievements are worked out from the guild's shared history (kills, deaths, raid nights and loot), so every guildmate sees the same ones, and nothing extra needs setting up.

## Seeing them

- **PvP**, **Hunts**, **Raids**, **Loot** and **Social** show tiles for each achievement: gold when earned, with the date, or your progress so far (like 12 / 25). Each group has several pages: use the arrows next to your name, or the mouse wheel.
- **Guild** lists achievements earned across the guild, newest first, the guild's goals, and who has the most. Click a name to see all of theirs.
- From anyone's **player card**, click the achievements button to see theirs.
- When a guildmate earns one while you're online, you hear about it. Turn that off in **Settings > Alerts** (Achievements earned).

Streaks are killing blows in a row without dying to an enemy player, each within an hour of the last. Grey kills never count. Night Owl, Early Bird, Weekend Warrior and the one-day achievements go by your own computer's clock. Rival guild achievements count kills of guilds your officers have marked as rivals (Wanted tab, Guilds view). Crafting achievements come from the recipes you've shared by opening your profession windows. Levels, DKP and alts count as they are now, so they have no date. Perfect Attendance counts finished months. Full Clear needs a raid whose boss count Warcrest knows (Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair, among others).

## Titles

Achievements marked with a title let you show it after your name, like **Lyra the Avenger**. Click the **Title** button at the top right of the Achievements tab: it lists every title, with the ones you've earned ready to pick and the rest showing which achievement gives them. Guildmates see it on your player card and on the Roster. You can only pick titles you've earned, and **No title** turns it off.

## Good to know

- Once your addon has seen an achievement earned, it keeps it, even if an officer clears the kill log or a DKP season archives old history.
- Achievements from before you installed the addon count too, as far back as the guild's shared history goes.
