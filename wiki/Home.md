# Warcrest

**Website:** https://warcrest-addon.com/ (with a contact form for bugs and ideas) · **Download:** https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/warcrest · **Discord:** https://discord.gg/qeSMwQWyPR

Guild tools for World of Warcraft: Forever: raid sign-ups, a full DKP system with live loot auctions and wishlists, PvP kill alerts, a wanted list of gankers, achievements and player cards, all shared automatically between guildmates.

Warcrest was made for **<The Imperium>** and used to carry its name. It now works for any guild; members of <The Imperium> still see their own emblem. Coming from The Imperium? Just update: your settings and history move over by themselves.

## What it does

- **PvP kill alerts** with killing blows and assists, and a monthly **Top Head Hunters** board.
- A **Wanted list** of enemy players who keep killing guild members, with guild-wide sighting alerts.
- **Raid sign-ups** by role, reminders, one-click invites, standby and no-show tracking.
- **DKP** for attendance, hourly play and boss kills, spent in **live loot auctions** (open or hidden bids), with free rolls, decay, alts, corrections and seasons.
- **Loot wishlists** (Need or Want), shown to officers and bidders when an item is auctioned, and a **loot council** for guilds that vote on loot.
- **Raid groups planner:** groups and bench planned from the sign-ups, and the raid arranged in one click.
- A **war board** of enemy guilds, with **rival guilds** marked by officers and alerts when a group of them is spotted.
- **Attunements** for every raid, **raid assignments** for each boss, and **wipe tracking** with best kill times.
- **Guild polls** and **crafting orders** from the crafters directory.
- **Guild goals** for the week or month, **315 achievements** with titles, and a **crafters directory** to find who can make what.
- **Bounties** on gankers (officers turn them on), and **alert banners** with a look and sound for each kind of alert.
- In **English, German, French and Spanish**.
- **Achievements and titles** earned from kills, streaks, raids and loot, and **player cards** for every guild member.
- **Guild tools:** roster management for officers, an **inactive members** list, level-up celebrations, death marks on the map, and an Officer Log.
