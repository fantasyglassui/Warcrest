Alerts

The whole guild hears it as it happens.

A banner for each kind of news

Kills, deaths, gankers, rivals, bosses, raids, loot and achievements each get their own colour, icon and sound. Click a banner to open what it's about.

87 alert sounds, or your own

Pick a sound for each kind of alert from Warcrest's own, the game's or SharedMedia's, or drop your own .ogg and .mp3 files in a folder.

Quiet when it matters

Hold alerts in dungeons, raids or combat, and read them in chat once you're out. Officers choose what the guild hears; everyone can turn off what they don't want.

What's inside

Built around how a guild actually plays.

Everything is kept in the guild's shared record and passed between guildmates' addons, so a member who was offline catches up when they log in, and nothing lives on one officer's computer.

World PvP

Kills, gankers and guild wars

  • Kill alerts with killing blows and assists, and a monthly Top Head Hunters board. Grey kills never count.
  • A Wanted list of enemy players who keep killing guildmates. When anyone spots one, everyone is alerted and the spot goes on the map.
  • The war board keeps score against every enemy guild you've met. Officers mark rivals, and the guild is alerted when a group of them shows up.
  • Streaks, revenge kills and bounties paid in DKP.
Raids and loot

Sign-ups to the last bid

  • Raid sign-ups by role, reminders, one-click invites, standby and no-show tracking.
  • DKP for being on time, every hour and every boss, spent in live loot auctions with open or hidden bids.
  • Free rolls, decay, alts, seasons, a loot council, and wishlists shown to bidders when an item comes up.
  • A raid groups planner built from the sign-ups, and raid progress for every boss, with wipes and best kill times.
  • Attunements for every raid, and raid assignments: each player is told their job when the boss is pulled.
Guild life

Events, polls and crafters

  • Guild events for everything that isn't a raid: PvP nights, world bosses, dungeon runs, meetings. Anyone can plan one.
  • Member profiles with spec, role, play times and birthday. Item level fills itself in from your gear.
  • The crafters directory: search what the guild can make and send a crafting order to whoever makes it.
  • Guild polls: ask a question and the guild votes.
  • Player cards for every guildmate, online or not, with their kills, nemesis, DKP and attendance.
Achievements and goals

Something to chase every week

  • 161 achievements from the guild's shared history, so everything you did before installing counts too.
  • 67 titles to show after your name, in chat, in kill alerts and on your player card.
  • Guild goals that officers set for the week or the month, with progress everyone can see.
  • Kept with the guild, so they're never lost with one computer's saved data.

Getting started

Install it, log in, done.

Warcrest shares with every guildmate who has it. The more of the guild installs it, the more it sees.

Install from CurseForge

In the CurseForge app, search for Warcrest and click Install. Make sure it's ticked in the AddOns list on the character screen.

Open it

Click the emblem on your minimap, or type /warcrest (or /wc). It catches up on the guild's history by itself.

Officers: set the guild up

Pick which alerts the guild hears, who counts as an officer and whether you use DKP in Settings > Alerts, then Save for whole guild.

Questions

Good to know

Does everyone need it?

No. Guildmates without it still see achievements posted in guild chat. Everyone with it shares kills, sign-ups and DKP with each other.

What if I lose my saved data?

Your achievements and the guild's history are kept by everyone's addon, so they come back from your guildmates.

Which languages?

English, German, French and Spanish. It follows your game's language, or pick one in Settings > General.

Something isn't counting

Type /warcrest check to see why an achievement or kill has or hasn't counted.

Found a bug, or have an idea?

Come and talk on the Warcrest Discord: get help setting your guild up, hear about new versions first, and meet other guilds using it. For a bug, type /warcrest report in game and paste what it gives you.
