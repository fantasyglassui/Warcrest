# Warcrest Discord, #announcements

**Warcrest 4.2.0 is out:** get your whole guild on it.

- Officers: **Guild > Who has Warcrest > Invite the guild** posts a short message in guild chat (you see it first; nothing goes out until you click Post).
- Everyone: **Share Warcrest** on Home, or `/warcrest share`, for a one-line message to send a friend or another guild.
- New players get a short picture tour when they first open it.

Know a guild that would use it? The website's new "For guild leaders" section explains it in 30 seconds: https://warcrest-addon.com/#leaders
