# Blizzard forums (US), WoW: Forever General Discussion

**Title:** Warcrest: a free guild add-on for WoW Forever (raids, DKP, PvP, polls)

Hi all,

I make **Warcrest**, a free guild add-on for WoW: Forever. It started as the in-house tool for my guild, <The Imperium>, and now works for any guild.

Everything is shared between the guildmates who have it, with no website to log in to and nothing to host:

- **Raids:** sign-ups by role, a group planner, attendance, boss kills and wipes, and a recap at the end of the night.
- **DKP and loot:** live loot auctions with open or hidden bids, wishlists, and a loot council if you prefer one.
- **PvP:** kill alerts for the guild, a monthly leaderboard, and a wanted list of gankers that warns everyone when one is spotted.
- **Guild life:** events anyone can plan, polls, 161 achievements with titles, member profiles and a crafter directory.

If you lead a guild, setting it up takes about five minutes: install it, pick which ranks run it and whether you use DKP, then click **Invite the guild** to post a short message in guild chat. Warcrest never posts or whispers anything on its own.

It's in English, German, French and Spanish.

- Website and a short tour: https://warcrest-addon.com/

Happy to answer questions here, and feedback is very welcome.
