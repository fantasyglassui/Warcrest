# Warcrest

**Guild tools for World of Warcraft: Forever.** Raids, guild events, DKP, loot, PvP alerts, achievements, member profiles and officer tools in one window, shared automatically with every guildmate who has it. Members don't have to set anything up: install it and it starts syncing. Built to stay fast in big guilds with a long history.

**Website:** [warcrest-addon.com](https://warcrest-addon.com/) (also in [Deutsch](https://warcrest-addon.com/de/), [Français](https://warcrest-addon.com/fr/) and [Español](https://warcrest-addon.com/es/)): a tour of every section, what's new, [the full changelog](https://warcrest-addon.com/changelog/), all 315 achievements, and a contact form for bugs and ideas.

**[Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/qeSMwQWyPR)** for help with setting it up, news about new versions, and other guilds using Warcrest.

Formerly **The Imperium**. Updating keeps all your settings and history.

## For guild leaders: your whole guild on it in five minutes
Warcrest is free, and everything is shared between the guildmates who have it. There's no website to log in to and nothing to host. The more of your guild installs it, the more it does.
1. **Install it** from CurseForge.
2. **Run the setup:** the welcome window asks which ranks run Warcrest and whether your guild uses DKP. Save it, and every member gets the same settings.
3. **Post the message in guild chat:** in **Guild > Who has Warcrest**, click **Invite the guild**. You see the message first, and it's posted only when you click Post. Warcrest never posts, whispers or advertises on its own.

New members see a short picture tour when they first open it, and officers get one reminder on Home while fewer than half the active members have it.

## One window for the guild
**Home** shows what's happening on one page: the next raid and your job in it, your DKP, the open poll (vote right there), PvP this week and guild news. The side menu groups everything else into **Guild**, **Events**, **Raids**, **Loot**, **PvP**, **Achievements** and **Officers**, each with its views along the top.

## Raids
- **Sign-ups:** officers schedule raids. Members sign up as Tank, Healer or DPS and get a reminder before the raid starts.
- **Raid groups planner:** put the sign-ups into groups and on the bench, or let **Auto-fill** do it. At raid time, **Arrange raid** moves everyone into their planned group in one click.
- **Calendar** (under Events): the month's raids, raid nights, boss kills and guild events at a glance.
- **Progress:** boss kills are recorded for the guild, raid by raid. Guild firsts and full clears are announced to everyone.
- **Wipe tracking:** every boss pull is recorded, kill or wipe. See the wipes and the **best kill time** for each boss, and hear when the raid beats it.
- **Attunements:** who's attuned for Molten Core, Onyxia, Blackwing Lair and Naxxramas, and who has the UBRS and Scholomance keys. Sign-ups who aren't attuned are flagged.
- **Raid assignments:** officers write who does what on each boss and post it to raid chat. Everyone sees their own job, and is reminded of it when the boss is pulled.
- **Raid night recap:** when the raid night ends, the guild gets a summary of the bosses, loot, attendance and the DKP each person earned.
- **One-click invites,** standby, attendance and no-show tracking.

## Guild events
- **Everything that isn't a raid:** PvP nights, world boss hunts, dungeon runs, guild meetings and get-togethers. **Anyone in the guild can plan one.**
- Guildmates answer **Going, Maybe or Can't go**, hear about new events as they're planned, and get a reminder before the ones they're going to.
- Whoever planned it (or an officer) can **invite everyone going** in one click and **take attendance** from their group.
- Events show on the calendar alongside raids, each kind in its own colour.
- **Guild polls:** ask the guild a question with a few answers, and everyone with Warcrest votes. Officers can let members start polls too.

## Loot
- **DKP:** earned for being on time, for every hour of a raid night and for every boss kill. You spend it in **live loot auctions**, with open or hidden bids.
  - Free rolls when nobody bids.
  - Weekly decay and seasons.
  - Alts share their main's DKP.
  - Every change can be undone.
- **Loot council:** vote on who gets an item instead. Officers see each candidate's wishlist, attendance and recent loot, then award it. This works with or without DKP.
- **Wishlists:** mark the items you **Need** or **Want**. Officers and bidders see who wants an item when it drops.
- **DKP is optional:** guilds that don't use it can turn it off, and everything else keeps working.

## PvP
- **Kill alerts:** when a guildmate kills an enemy player, the guild hears about it, killing blows and assists included.
- **Top Head Hunters:** a monthly board of the guild's top PvP killers. The champion is announced on the 1st.
- **Kill streaks and revenge:** the guild hears about hot streaks, from Killing Spree at 3 kills to Godlike at 20 and Beyond Godlike at 30, ended streaks, and paybacks on a guildmate's killer.
- **Wanted list:** enemy players who keep ganking your guild. When anyone spots one, everyone is alerted and the spot goes on the map.
- **War board:** the score against every enemy guild you've met. Officers mark **rival guilds**: they're listed first, the guild is alerted when a group of them is spotted, and the weekly recap says how the war went.
- **Bounties:** officers can put a reward on a ganker's head, and the guildmate who kills them claims it. Bounties are off until an officer turns them on.
- **Deaths on the map:** recent guild deaths and wanted sightings are marked on the world map, and inside **OpenMap** too. Click a mark to set a **TomTom** waypoint.

## Alerts that stand out
Guild alerts drop in at the top of the screen as banners. Each kind has its own colour, icon and sound: a kill, a wanted ganker spotted, a boss down, a bounty, an achievement.
- Click a banner to jump to the matching tab.
- **Quiet mode** holds alerts during dungeons, raids or combat.
- Choose which alerts you see, or switch the banners to the classic raid warning text or to chat only.

## 87 alert sounds, and your own
Every kind of alert has its own sound, and you can change any of them in **Settings > Sounds**.
- **87 sound effects made for Warcrest**, in eight kinds:
  - **Horns & fanfares:** war horn, battle horn, royal fanfare, rally cry.
  - **Bells & chimes:** church bells, wind chimes, music box, singing bowl, death toll.
  - **Drums:** war drums, taiko, timpani roll.
  - **Combat:** sword clash, axe chop, shield block, arrow volley, battle cry.
  - **Magic:** arcane blast, holy light, frost shimmer, shadow pulse, portal.
  - **Alerts:** siren, klaxon, countdown, radar ping.
  - **Nature:** thunder, rain, wind, wolf howl, raven.
  - **Music:** harp, lute, flute, organ, marimba.
- The game's own sounds too, and sounds your other add-ons share (SharedMedia) if you turn them on.
- **Add your own .ogg or .mp3 files:** drop them in a WarcrestSounds folder and add them by name. Updates never touch them.
- Hear any sound with one click, search by name or kind, and pick which volume channel alerts play on.

## Achievements, profiles and player cards
- **315 achievements** across PvP, hunts, raids, loot and social life, with **78 titles** you can show after your name. Everything you've already done counts. They're kept with the guild, so they're never lost with one computer's saved data.
- **Guild goals:** officers set targets for the week or month (enemy players killed, bosses, raid nights, events and more). Everyone sees the progress, and the guild hears when one is reached.
- **Crafters** (in the Guild section): search what the guild can make, see who's online to make it, and send them a **crafting order** (say if you're bringing the materials). They accept, decline or mark it done, and you hear about it. Recipes are shared just by opening your profession windows.
- **Member profiles:** your main spec and role, the days and times you play, your birthday and a short note. **Your item level is added automatically** from your gear.
- **Guild profiles** (in the Guild section): everyone's spec, role, item level and play times in one list, sortable for a quick gear check.
- **Birthdays and anniversaries** are celebrated with the guild.
- **Player cards:** click any guildmate's name, online or offline, to see their profile, rank and guild, kills, deaths, nemesis, best streak, DKP, attendance and loot in one place.
- **Weekly recap:** after the Tuesday reset, a summary of the guild's week in chat.

## For officers
- **Roster tools:** search, notes, promote, demote, remove, invite, and the Message of the Day.
- **Officer log:** promotions, joins, leaves and note changes.
- **Inactive members:** who hasn't logged in for 14, 30, 60 or 90 days, with their rank and last raid.
- **Officers without setup:** every rank that can read officer chat counts as an officer in Warcrest. The Guild Master can pick other ranks.
- **Guild-wide settings:** choose which alerts the whole guild hears and which ranks manage Warcrest.
- **Addon check:** see who has Warcrest and who's out of date.
- **Recruiting:** a recruit list shared by every officer (New, Contacted, Trial, Joined, Declined), trial tracking with raid and event attendance, players who whisper about joining, and a recruiting message you post with one click.

## Works with
- **OpenMap:** guild death marks and wanted sightings show on OpenMap's map as well as the game's own.
- **TomTom:** click a mark on the map to set a waypoint to it.

Both are optional. Warcrest works fine without them.

## Accessibility
Settings > Accessibility (or **/warcrest access**): bigger text, colour-blind friendly colours, high contrast, and alert banners that stay longer or until you dismiss them, with no sliding or fading. All off until you turn them on.

## Languages
English, **Deutsch**, **Français** and **Español**, help included. Warcrest follows your game's language, or you can pick one in Settings.

## Getting started
1. Install Warcrest. Ask your guildmates to install it too, because it works best when everyone has it.
2. The first time you log in, the **Getting started** checklist opens: sign up for a raid, fill in your profile, share your recipes and pick a title. Each step ticks itself off. Open it again with **/warcrest start**.
3. Officers: **Guild setup** in that window sets whether your guild uses DKP and which alerts it hears.

Works with WoW: Forever's addon limits: during boss fights and PvP matches the game blocks addon messages, so Warcrest holds what it has to send and sends it when the fight ends.

Stuck? Click **Help** at the bottom of the side menu, or type **/warcrest help**.

## Bugs, ideas and questions
Type `/warcrest report` in game and paste what it gives you, so the problem can be found quickly. Ask on the [Warcrest Discord](https://discord.gg/qeSMwQWyPR), or use the **contact form** on the [Warcrest website](https://warcrest-addon.com/#contact). It goes straight to the developer, and you get the reply by email. In game, `/warcrest website` shows the address, ready to copy.
